Hey, welcome to GGA! 👋

Before you jump in, do one quick thing — tap the **Set Up Your Profile** button (or type **/profile** in any channel) and fill out your profile. It only takes a minute:

1. Golf+ username (optional)
2. Days you play, plus your usual playing-time window (Eastern Time)
3. Golf+ handicap
4. Preferred tour — ProTour, Accessible, or Both

It helps us match you with the right people and teams. You can update it anytime by tapping the button or running **/profile** again.

Glad you're here. ⛳
