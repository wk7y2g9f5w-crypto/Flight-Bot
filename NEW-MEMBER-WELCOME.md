Hey, welcome to GGA! 👋

Before you jump in, do one quick thing — type **/profile** in any channel and fill out your profile. It only takes a minute:

1. Golf+ username
2. Timezone
3. Availability (when you usually play)
4. Golf+ handicap
5. Preferred tour — ProTour, Accessible, or Both

It helps us match you with the right people and teams. You can update it anytime by running **/profile** again.
